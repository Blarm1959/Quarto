How to play

Choose a piece for your opponent. They place it on any empty square, then choose the next piece for you.

Make a row, column or diagonal of four pieces sharing at least one attribute: tall, short, round, square, light, dark, solid or hole.

Piece placement and win detection are added in the next versions.
